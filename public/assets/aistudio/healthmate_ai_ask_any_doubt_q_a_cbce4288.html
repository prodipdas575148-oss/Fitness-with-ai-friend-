Common doubts

Can I use lemon juice on dark spots, and how do I fade them safely?

auto_awesome

Clear answer

Verified guide

No—skip lemon juice. Its high acidity can irritate your skin barrier and cause sunburn or darker spots in daylight.

Step-by-step safe solution
  1. Wash with a gentle, fragrance-free cleanser morning and night.
  2. Apply a mild niacinamide (2–5%) or vitamin C serum once daily.
  3. Wear broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning so dark spots do not deepen.

When to see a doctor: If spots change shape, bleed, or do not fade after 8 weeks.
