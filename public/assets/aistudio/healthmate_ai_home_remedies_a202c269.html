Safe home care for everyday problems

Evidence-based remedies for health, skin, hair, and digestion—plus common myths to skip.

Sore throat & cough

Health

Safe care: Gargle with warm salt water (½ tsp salt in 1 cup warm water) or sip warm water with 1 tsp honey (for ages 1+).

block Avoid: Never give honey to babies under 12 months, and skip very hot steam bowls near children.

Acne & skin redness

Skin

Safe care: Apply a cool, damp green-tea compress for 5 minutes or a thin layer of pure, fragrance-free aloe vera gel.

block Avoid: Lemon juice, toothpaste, baking soda, or harsh sugar scrubs—they irritate and burn skin.

Dandruff & itchy scalp

Hair

Safe care: Rinse with lukewarm water, soothe with light aloe gel 20 minutes before washing, and massage gently with fingertips.

block Avoid: Heavy oil left overnight on active dandruff or undiluted vinegar, which can worsen flaking.

Bloating & mild indigestion

Digestion

Safe care: Sip warm ginger or peppermint tea slowly and take an easy 10-minute walk after eating.

block Avoid: Lying flat right after meals or taking unverified herbal powders.

Dry, flaky skin

Skin

Safe care: Use a lukewarm colloidal oatmeal soak and seal damp skin with a plain, fragrance-free moisturizer.

block Avoid: Very hot showers, scented soaps, and rough loofah scrubbing.

Mild tension headache

Health

Safe care: Rest in a quiet dim room with a cool forehead cloth and drink 2 glasses of water slowly.

block Avoid: Applying strong essential oils near eyes or ignoring sudden severe pain.
