Possible common causes

Mild headaches with fatigue often come from dehydration, screen eye strain, missed meals, or poor sleep.

Safe home care
  • check_circle Drink 2 glasses of water slowly and rest your eyes.
  • check_circle Have a light, balanced meal with protein and fruit.
When to see a doctor

Check with a doctor if the headache lasts more than 3 days, worsens suddenly, or comes with high fever, stiff neck, or blurred vision.
