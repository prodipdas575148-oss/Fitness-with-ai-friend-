Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)

Pain & Fever

Common uses: Mild fever, headache, and everyday body aches.

Side effects & precautions: Usually gentle at label doses, but taking too much can harm the liver. Check cold/flu labels for hidden paracetamol and avoid alcohol.

Cetirizine

Allergy

Common uses: Seasonal sneezing, runny nose, itchy eyes, and mild hives.

Side effects & precautions: May cause mild drowsiness or dry mouth. Use caution when driving and ask a doctor if pregnant, nursing, or taking kidney medicines.

Ketoconazole Shampoo

Skin & Scalp

Common uses: Dandruff flaking and itchy scalp caused by excess scalp yeast.

Side effects & precautions: External scalp use only; may dry hair slightly. Keep out of eyes and ask a doctor if redness or irritation worsens.

Antibiotics & Steroids

Doctor-only

When a doctor recommends them: Only after confirming a bacterial infection or specific inflammatory skin condition.

Why never to self-medicate: Antibiotics do not treat viruses, and using steroid creams on the face without guidance can thin skin and worsen acne.

emergency Swelling of lips or throat, trouble breathing, severe rash, or taking more than the label dose? Seek urgent medical care immediately.
