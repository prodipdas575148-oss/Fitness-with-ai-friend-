Gentle care steps

Use a mild fragrance-free cleanser, a simple moisturizer, and broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning. Avoid scrubbing, picking, or applying lemon juice or toothpaste.

When to see a doctor

Visit a dermatologist if spots are painful, spreading quickly, oozing, or not improving after 4–6 weeks of gentle care.

emergency Sudden facial or lip swelling, severe rash with fever, or trouble breathing? Seek urgent medical care right away.
