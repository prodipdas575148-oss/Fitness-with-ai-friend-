Healthy weight & fat loss

Safe home habits and simple daily exercises for steady, lasting fat loss—no crash diets.

verified_user Healthy fat loss comes from balanced meals, daily movement, and good sleep. No drink or remedy melts fat overnight.

Safe home habits

Water before meals & green tea

Drink 1 glass of plain or warm lemon water 20 minutes before meals to support fullness, and swap sugary drinks for unsweetened green tea.

Fiber & protein first

Start meals with vegetables, lentils, or soaked chia seeds plus protein (eggs, yogurt, tofu) to curb cravings naturally.

block Avoid: Laxative "slimming" teas, unverified fat-burner pills, or skipping meals—they cause water loss and rebound weight gain.

Daily exercises

1 / 4 done
  • Brisk walking 30 min
    Easy on joints; great after meals to support steady fat burn.
    Tip: Walk at a pace where you can still speak short sentences, swing your arms naturally, and wear cushioned shoes.
  • Marching or jacks 3 × 45s
    Raises heart rate at home with zero equipment.
    Tip: Land softly on the balls of your feet, or step side-to-side without jumping if your knees feel sensitive.
  • Bodyweight squats 3 × 12
    Strengthens legs and boosts daily calorie burn.
    Tip: Keep chest lifted, weight on your heels, and knees tracking over your toes—not caving inward.
  • Forearm plank hold 3 × 30s
    Tones deep core muscles and supports back posture.
    Tip: Keep hips level with shoulders—do not let your lower back sag—and drop to your knees if needed.
emergency Stop exercising right away if you feel chest pain, dizziness, or trouble breathing. Check with a doctor first if you have heart, joint, or blood pressure conditions.
