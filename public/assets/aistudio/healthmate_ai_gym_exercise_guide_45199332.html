Today’s workout

1 / 5 done
  • Treadmill incline walk 20 min
    Incline 6%, steady pace—low impact for fat burn.
    Form tip: Stand tall without leaning on the handrails, land softly mid-foot, and breathe steadily through your nose and mouth.
  • Dumbbell goblet squat 3 × 12
    Hold weight at chest; keep back straight and knees aligned.
    Form tip: Push hips back as if sitting in a chair, keep heels flat on the floor, and inhale on the way down.
  • Lat pulldown machine 3 × 12
    Pull bar smoothly to upper chest for back and posture.
    Form tip: Squeeze your shoulder blades down and back rather than swinging your torso; avoid pulling behind the neck.
  • Dumbbell chest press 3 × 10
    Lower slowly with elbows at 45°; breathe out as you press.
    Form tip: Keep both feet flat on the floor and wrists straight; stop just before locking out elbows at the top.
  • Seated leg press 3 × 12
    Push through heels; avoid locking knees at the top.
    Form tip: Keep your lower back pressed against the seat pad and control the return over 2 seconds.

Smart gym habits

Warm up for 5 minutes before lifting, start with light weights to learn form, and rest 60 seconds between sets.

emergency Stop right away if you feel sharp joint pain, chest pain, or dizziness. Ask a doctor before heavy lifting if you have high BP or back issues.
